## The Pitch
Do you like listening to music? - Most likely.  
Do you wanna make music?? - Surely.  
Don't wanna drop your life's savings on a synth that does but one thing?  
Then you need InstroPad(1)! My fully custom music creation tool and macro pad.

## The Features
- OLED display
- Rotary + push button encoder - for all your knobby interface needs
- 3x3 fully customisable pad layout - that's 9 whole pads!
- Inspired by classics such as the TR 808 and 303
- Full portability - make music anywhere!
- Fully custom firmware
- Fully custom software - coming soon(tm)
- Specifically designed M3 mounting holes that allow for adjustments in accordance to manufacturing tolerances
- Standard numpad inputs - the switches act as a numpad 1-9 and the push encoder acts as an enter, how useful!
